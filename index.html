    Safe to Trade

    A hygiene rating tells people about the last inspection. This tells them about every day since.

    Shield Safety is the certification body for Safe to Trade, the independent mark for businesses that put safety first. Certification means unannounced audits, annual rechecks, continuous monitoring and a public listing consumers can check.

    ISO 17065

    Certification and commercial activities kept separate under the international standard for certifying bodies.

    4 boards

    Independent governance, technical standards, stakeholder forum and impartiality committee.

    Unannounced

    Audits arrive when your guests do. That is what makes the mark worth something.

    Public

    Approved businesses are listed on safetotrade.com, with consumer feedback.
