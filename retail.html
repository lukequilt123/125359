How a retail programme runs

Design, calibrate, audit, improve.

Retail estates are audited on a cycle rather than certified once. The sequence below is how Tesco's programme was built.

Month 1

Audit standard designed with you

We build the standard around your policies, procedures and codes of practice, then agree scoring and risk categories.

Month 1 to 2

Auditor calibration

Environmental Health graduates are calibrated against the standard through a quality assurance programme and KPIs.

Month 2

Software and scheduling live

RiskProof is configured for the estate. Audits are scheduled by risk so resource lands where it counts.

Ongoing

Audit round

Risk-based, coaching-style inspections. Findings and actions logged on the spot and visible to store, region and head office.

Ongoing

Non-conformance tracking

Every action tracked to closure. Trends across formats and regions surface what to fix at policy level.

Quarterly

Review and refine

Programme review with your account manager. The standard evolves with emerging trends and your own business changes.

FAQ

Questions retailers ask

Do you only audit food?

No. Programmes cover food safety, health and safety and store standards. Tesco's programme supports People Safety, Food Safety and Regulatory teams.

Can the audit follow our own codes of practice?

Yes. The audit standard is designed around your policies, procedures and practices, so scores reflect what your business asks of colleagues.

How do you keep auditors consistent across a national estate?

Through a full quality assurance programme and KPIs that calibrate every auditor against the standard.

Is Safe to Trade relevant to retail?

Safe to Trade certification is available to food business operators, including in-store food service. Ask us about scope for your formats.

What software do store teams use?

RiskProof. Non-conformance tracking, risk-based scheduling and estate-wide reporting in one login, with records ready for any inspection.
