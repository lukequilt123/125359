FAQ

RiskProof questions

Which areas of safety does RiskProof cover?

Food safety, health and safety, fire safety, incident logging, certifications, training, risk assessments, documents and policy, in modules you switch on as needed.

Does it support multi-location operations?

Yes. Group-wide dashboards and per-site reports, with standardised routines across every location.

How does expert support work?

Log an incident or question from the platform and an Environmental Health Practitioner responds. Every case is tracked for due diligence.

Is it secure?

RiskProof is Cyber Essentials Plus certified and ESG-aligned. Fully digital, secure and paperless.

How long does adoption take?

Configuration, remote or onsite training and onboarding support typically have teams live within the first three weeks.
