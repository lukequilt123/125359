FAQ

Questions operators ask

What is Shield Assure Hospitality?

A complete safety and risk management solution for hotels, restaurants, pubs and leisure venues. It combines Safe to Trade certification, RiskProof software and expert support to protect reputation, streamline daily operations and support growth.

How does it keep us audit-ready?

Real-time dashboards, automated documentation and expert guidance keep every venue prepared for audits and inspections, and help you maintain Safe to Trade approval.

Does it guarantee Safe to Trade approval?

No. It provides the tools, support and documentation needed for independent audits. Certification is impartial, which is exactly why it is worth having.

What technology powers it?

RiskProof, our award-winning platform, delivering real-time risk management, streamlined documentation and actionable insight for hotel and restaurant teams.

How does it help with staff training and turnover?

Simple training modules and onboarding support make it easy to bring new hires up to standard quickly and keep safety consistent across sites.

How quickly can we implement?

Fast. Remote training, onboarding support and bespoke configuration minimise disruption. Most groups have RiskProof live within the first three weeks.
