What certification involves

More than a badge. A promise you keep every day.

A hygiene rating reflects one visit. Safe to Trade is maintained, which is why guests, partners and regulators can rely on it.

Unannounced audits

Tailored to the complexity of the business. Auditors arrive when customers do.

Annual recheck

Certification is renewed every year, with continuous monitoring in between.

Public listing

Approved businesses are listed on safetotrade.com, with consumer feedback.

Media pack

Signage, digital assets and the mark itself, so the standard is visible before anyone walks in.

Impartiality

Governed for independence.

Shield Safety operates under ISO/IEC 17065, the international standard for certifying bodies. Certification activity is kept separate from commercial interests, and four independent bodies oversee it. UKAS accreditation has been formally applied for.

Independent Governance Board

Oversight of the certification scheme as a whole.

Technical Standards Committee

Owns the standard and how it evolves.

Stakeholder Engagement Forum
